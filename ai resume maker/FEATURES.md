### 6. ATS Compatibility Analysis
- **What it does:** Scans for ATS-blocking issues
- **Checks for:**

  - Keyword density
  - Action verb usage
  - Formatting issues
  - Section completeness
- **Provides:**
  - Compatibility score
  - Specific issues list
  - Fix recommendations
  - Best practices validation

### 7. Keyword Matcher
- **What it does:** Matches your resume to job descriptions
- **How to use:** Paste job description, click "Match Keywords"
- **Shows:**
  - Match percentage
  - Matched keywords
  - Missing keywords
  - Keyword suggestions
  - Optimization tips

### 8. Resume Scoring System
- **Real-time scoring** (0-100)
- **Factors considered:**

- **Updates automatically** as you edit
